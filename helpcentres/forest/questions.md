# Questions — "Your platform and Session Planner"

Add these as **native Question items** in the unit, after the SCORM lesson. Not inside the
SCORM: see the note at the bottom for why.

Each one exists because getting it wrong produces a support ticket or a misread report. They are
not comprehension checks on the text — they are the three misunderstandings we already know are
coming.

---

### 1. Session Planner opens in a new tab and asks you to log in. Which password do you use?

- A. The same one you use for this platform
- **B. Your Session Planner password, which is a separate account** ← correct
- C. You do not need one, it signs you in automatically

**If correct:** Right. Two Coaches' Voice platforms, two accounts — there is no single sign-on
between them, so it is worth saving both somewhere you can find them.

**If wrong:** Session Planner is also Coaches' Voice, but it has its own account. There is no
single sign-on, so your password there may well be different from this one. Save both.

---

### 2. You spend a week dipping in and out of *Game Model: Reference*. What happens to your progress?

- A. It goes up as you watch each clip
- **B. Nothing — reference material is not tracked** ← correct
- C. It counts towards your CPD but not towards the module

**If correct:** Right, and that is deliberate. Use it as often as you like; it will never affect
your record. *The 4 moments* is the one you complete.

**If wrong:** Reference material never moves your progress and appears in no report — by design,
so you can dip into it freely without it looking like activity. *The 4 moments* is the module you
complete; *Reference* is the one you come back to.

---

### 3. You are halfway through a video when you have to stop. What happens when you come back?

- A. It picks up from where you paused
- **B. That lesson starts again from the beginning** ← correct
- C. The whole module resets

**If correct:** Right. Your place saves lesson by lesson, not inside one — so stop between
lessons where you can.

**If wrong:** Progress saves lesson by lesson. A video you abandon part-way through does not keep
its position, so you will watch that one again. Short version: stop between lessons, not inside one.

---

## Why native questions rather than questions inside the SCORM

- **They show up in reporting.** Gav can see who answered what, per coach. A score buried in a
  SCORM tells him a number and nothing else.
- **Archie or Carly can edit them** in the platform. A question inside the SCORM needs the package
  rebuilt and re-uploaded for a comma.
- **They feed the module's own Percentage To Pass**, and later the end-of-onboarding assessment
  Gav asked for as module 5.
- It keeps a clean split: **the SCORM is the content, the questions are the check.** The SCORM
  then needs no scoring logic at all, which is one less thing to get wrong in an LMS iframe.
